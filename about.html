Our story

A Little Stand With Big Vibes

Why Sips?

Every drink stand in town was built for grown-ups. So we built one for kids.

Sips started in Sutherlin, Oregon with one simple idea: kids deserve their own special drink stop. A place where the flavors are fun, the cups have their name on them, and every visit feels like a tiny celebration.

Today we mix every drink fresh to order — bright fruit flavors, crunchy ice, and just the right amount of fizz. Grown-ups are welcome too. (Honestly, they love it just as much.)
