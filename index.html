About Sips

Made in Sutherlin

Sips is a locally run drive-thru right on West Central Avenue in Sutherlin, Oregon. We opened in 2025 with a ribbon cutting alongside the Sutherlin Area Chamber, and we've been pouring for our neighbors ever since.

Swing through on your way to work, after school, or whenever you need something cold — we'll see you at the window.
